Met:

The Legendary MGI Band !

Not many people know that the Laboratory of Geo Information Science and Remote Sensing from Wageningen University was formed around the legendary MGI Band. The laboratory serves as inspiration for the songs that the legendary MGI Band performs. Many of these songs will sound familiar. Famous acts like the Beatles and John Mayer have performed songs that the legendary MGI Band also plays, with slightly different lyrics. On Wednesday evening, the legendary MGI Band will take you on a journey interpreting open source and related themes through music!
